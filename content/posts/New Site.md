Welcome to the new version of the Southwest Virginia Astronomy website!

Please bear with me as I update it and get content posted. This change was made due to the old version of the site being difficult to maintain and because a full Wordpress site is overkill for what this site is used for. 

If you are new here, welcome! This is intended as a place where more technical and long form posts can be made since most people are not going to be interested in reading long form content on Facebook. I post full tutorials, articles, and observing logs here as well as any recurring local events I know about.
