Touring The Galaxy 2026

August 25, 2026

Touring the Galaxy is a recurring public outreach astronomy event held at the Natural Tunnel State Park. All are welcome whether you have a telescope or not. Park volunteers will take you on a journey through space using your own two eyes and a telescope. You will hear descriptions of many objects and constellations in the night sky and get the chance to see things you have never seen before!

Event organisers: Ray O’Connor & Kate O’Connor

Location: Natural Tunnel State Park Gazebo

Price: FREE!
